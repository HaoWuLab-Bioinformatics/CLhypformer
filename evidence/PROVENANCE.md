# Input provenance

- `graph_accuracy.csv`, `graph_robustness.csv`, `graph_ablation.csv`: nonempty rows of the original graph-figure workbook's Accuracy, Robustness and Ablation sheets. Source spellings are retained. `original_figures/` contains the original figure images from the manuscript.
- `gcn_fixed_run8_coordinates.csv`: independently rerun from fixed split index 7 and seed 49 on the server; `gcn_fixed_run8.json` records the original checkpoint. `topology/`: archived Cora incidence matrices and the matching node/center label vector. The manuscript matrices agree with rounded densities of `0_run7` (initial), `1_run3` (Hypformer), and `2_run7` (CLhypformer).
- `predictions_neural.csv.gz`: full-model probabilities converted to argmax classes from `gse176078_full_20261017/runs/state_hyper_cl/` and validation-selected baseline predictions from `gse176078_caf_confirm_20261022/selected/`. `predictions_classical.csv.gz`: columns needed for evaluation from `gse176078_classic_annotation_20261018/manuscript_source_data/all_cell_predictions.csv.gz`. The duplicate full-model records are independently checked before replacement with the full set of neural records. Baseline methods share held-out cell indices and truth labels. `class_labels.csv`, `class_f1_percent.csv`, and `confusion_row_percent.csv` are the Figure 5 plotting/validation references.
- `state_coordinates_and_scores.csv`, `coordinate_audit.json`, `patient_gene_quintiles.csv`, and `plotted_*.csv`: source tables underlying the tumor-state figure. Row coordinates and patient-level quantities are frozen. Patient identifiers in generated analysis tables are replaced by package-local study codes and cell barcodes are omitted. The plot order field preserves the sort order for the published figure's seeded resampling.
- `all_patient_program_metrics.csv`, `paired_primary_summary.csv`: external program confirmation analysis, `gse176078_program_confirm_20261023/`. The former provides the patient-, program- and seed-level inputs; the latter is a fixed check for the recomputed aggregate. `revision_standard.csv` and `revision_strict.csv`: `revision_runs/single_cell/paired/{standard,strict}/external_patient_metrics.csv` with local patient codes.
- `cell_patient_codes.csv.gz`: package-local donor codes in source-atlas row order; codes preserve sorting for the original seeded patient bootstrap. `classification_paired_expected.json`: archived neural classification comparisons from the CAF confirmation study. `strict_fold1_predictions.csv.gz`: test label/prediction pairs for full and local models after panel B exclusion, both frozen seeds in fold 1.
- `paper_single_cell_ablation.csv`: ablation point estimates and interval bounds at the precision of the manuscript Results section.
